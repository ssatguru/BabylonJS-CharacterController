WebXR Demo of CharacterController for Babylonjs

Enable WebXR, then enter an immersive VR or AR session. Requires a secure context (HTTPS or localhost), and a compatible headset or the WebXR emulator browser extension.

In-session controls (thumbstick-primary locomotion):

  • Left thumbstick — walk / walk back / strafe
  • Left thumbstick press — run (fast)
  • Left trigger — jump
  • Right thumbstick — orbit the follow camera
  • Right A / B buttons — dolly camera out / in
  • Left X button — snap camera to avatar (dolly toggle)
  • Left A button — toggle first / third person

Append ?animType=ar to the URL to test the .babylon avatar with AnimationRanges instead of the .glb / AnimationGroups.
